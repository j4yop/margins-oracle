Advisors' Deliberations (Unabridged)

1. The Contrarian / Red Team Adversarial Risk

Verdict: Kill Collective Pool Buying immediately.

"Collective buying assumes five independent shopkeepers will mutually co-sign debt and inventory risk. In reality, if one shopkeeper defaults, the entire shipment is locked. For a 3-minute hackathon demo, collective buying looks like vaporware. Instead, focus on the social power asymmetry at the counter: distributors bully shopkeepers because bills are messy and handwritten. Giving the shopkeeper a definitive, statutory invoice breakdown with an automatic WhatsApp dispute gives them immediate leverage."

2. The First Principles Thinker Mechanics & Causality

Verdict: Close the transaction loop at point-of-settlement.

"The physical transaction takes 90 seconds. If a shopkeeper negotiates verbal savings from ₹280 to ₹252, but the distributor writes ₹280 on the carbon slip, the shopkeeper loses later during reconciliation. By generating a dynamic UPI QR intent for ₹252 on the spot, the payment transaction itself becomes the legal record. Pair this with Gemini invoice OCR for closed-loop financial verification."

3. The Expansionist / Strategist B2B Scale & Leverage

Verdict: Target the ₹45,000 Cr FMCG trade promotion leakage.

"FMCG giants like HUL, Amul, and ITC spend thousands of crores on trade schemes that distributors routinely pocket. By cross-referencing brand promotions against uploaded delivery slips, MARGINS transforms from a local kirana tool into the foundational compliance and audit intelligence rail for India's entire FMCG distribution ecosystem."

4. The Outsider / Domain Skeptic User Empathy

Verdict: Flour-on-fingers simplicity.

"A kirana owner in Madurai cannot parse a 15-column analytics grid. He needs three things: A red badge saying 'Overcharged ₹460', a green voice audio in Tamil whispering what to demand, and a green button opening WhatsApp with the delivery boy's pre-filled notice. Keep it ruthlessly tactile."

5. The Executor / Pragmatic Engineer Zero-Tax Implementation

Verdict: Native Web APIs with zero new backend infrastructure.

"We can implement both the FMCG Scheme Auditor and WhatsApp Dispute Rail directly inside our existing Next.js App Router: 1. Enrich `/api/audit/invoice` to return scheme discrepancy and pre-crafted WhatsApp message. 2. Render a 1-tap WhatsApp trigger (`wa.me/?text=...`) on the mobile camera page. 3. Fast, zero regressions, 100% build pass."
